Delete your Dukaan Tracker account

App by AdanLabs

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Dukaan Tracker and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and tap Delete Account at the bottom.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete Forever.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you can't open the app (for example you lost your phone or forgot your password), message us and we'll delete the account for you:

Email: YOUR CONTACT EMAIL
WhatsApp: YOUR WHATSAPP NUMBER

Include the phone number you registered with and the words "Delete my Dukaan Tracker account". We may ask you to confirm you own the number, for example with a code sent by SMS. We aim to finish within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login and account details
  • All your customers and their phone numbers and notes
  • All purchases, payments and balances
  • Your SMS reminder log

This cannot be undone. If you want a copy first, open Settings and use Export PDF or Export Excel (CSV) before deleting.

What may remain

Copies may stay in encrypted backups for a short time until they are overwritten. They are not used for anything else. Records that the law requires us to keep, if any, are kept only as long as required.
