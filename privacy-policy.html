Dukaan Tracker Privacy Policy

Published by AdanLabs · Effective 17 October 2026

Dukaan Tracker helps shop owners in Somalia record customer debts and payments. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Who we are

Dukaan Tracker is made by AdanLabs, an independent developer based in Mogadishu, Somalia. In this policy, "we" means AdanLabs.

Contact: YOUR CONTACT EMAIL · WhatsApp: YOUR WHATSAPP NUMBER

2. Information the app handles

WhatDetails
Your accountYour name or business name, your phone number (used as your login ID), and your password. Passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read your password.
Your business recordsThe customers you add (name, phone number, optional note) and the purchases and payments you record (amount, description, date), plus each customer's balance.
SMS remindersWhen you send a reminder, we process the customer's phone number and the message text. We keep a log (date, number, message, whether it was delivered) to enforce monthly limits and fix problems.
Password resetYour phone number and a one-time 6-digit code. The code is stored only in scrambled form and expires after 10 minutes.
SettingsYour business name and theme choice (light, dark or system).

We do not use advertising or analytics tools in the app, and we do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, save and show your records, and keep them backed up in the cloud.
  • To send the SMS reminders and password-reset codes you ask for.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse (for example SMS limits) and fix problems.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — cloud hosting for the database and sign-in. Your account and records are stored there.
  • Hormuud Telecommunications — delivers SMS messages. They receive the recipient's phone number and the message text.
  • Google — the app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your device's IP address. Google also distributes the app through Google Play.

When you export a CSV or PDF, or tap Call or WhatsApp, the app hands the data or phone number to the app you choose on your phone. What that app does is covered by its own policy.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Your customers' information

The customer details you enter belong to your business. You decide what to record and you are responsible for having a good reason to keep it and to message those customers. We handle that information only to provide the app to you.

6. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each shop can only see its own records. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password, do not share it, and keep your phone locked.

7. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete Account.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Deleting your account permanently removes your login, customers, transactions and SMS log. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a short time until they are overwritten, and are not used for anything else.

8. Your choices

  • See or take a copy: use Export PDF or Export Excel (CSV) in Settings.
  • Correct: edit a customer, or delete a mistaken entry, in the app.
  • Delete: as described above.
  • Questions or complaints: contact us using the details above.

9. Children

Dukaan Tracker is a business tool for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will also tell you in the app.
